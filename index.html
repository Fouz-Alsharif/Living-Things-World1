Grammar World

Interactive Grammar Lessons

Study the three grammar rules below, try the practice activities, and take the Final Grammar Challenge!

1

Simple Present Tense (زمن المضارع البسيط)

Used for habits, routines, and general scientific facts.

📌 Structure & Rules:

  • I / You / We / They + base verb (e.g., need, live, eat)
  • He / She / It + verb + s / es (e.g., needs, lives, eats)

Negatives & Questions:

Negative: do not / does not + base verb

Question: Do / Does + subject + base verb?

💡 Examples with Arabic Translation:

  • Plants need water.النباتات تحتاج إلى الماء.
  • A beetle lives in the garden.تعيش الخنفساء في الحديقة.
  • Lions eat meat.الأسود تأكل اللحوم.
  • She feeds her cat every day.هي تطعم قطتها كل يوم.

✏️ Interactive Practice: Simple Present

1. A butterfly _____ flowers.
2. Plants _____ sunlight.
3. _____ a beetle have 6 legs?
4. Lions _____ meat.
2

Passive Voice — Simple Present (المبني للمجهول)

Used when the action or receiver is more important than the doer.

📌 Structure & Rule:

Subject + am / is / are + Past Participle (V3)

  • Singular subject → is (e.g., Honey is produced...)
  • Plural subject → are (e.g., Animals are protected...)

💡 Active vs Passive Examples:

  • Active: People protect wild animals.
    Passive: Wild animals are protected by people.
  • Active: Farmers grow plants.
    Passive: Plants are grown by farmers.

🔄 Active to Passive Converter Exercise

Active: "People protect wild animals." → Convert to Passive below:

Wild animals by people.

✏️ Interactive Practice: Passive Voice

1. Animals _____ protected in parks.
2. Honey _____ produced by bees.
3

Where Clause (جملة الوصل المكانية Where)

Used to talk about or describe a place.

📌 Structure & Rule:

Place + where + subject + verb

نستخدم where للحديث عن المكان الذي يحدث فيه شيء أو تعيش فيه الكائنات.

💡 Nature Examples:

  • A garden is a place where plants grow.
  • A zoo is a place where people can see wild animals.
  • A pond is a place where frogs live.
  • A forest is a place where animals find food.

✏️ Interactive Practice: Where Clause

1. A forest is a place _____ wild animals live.
2. A garden is a place where plants _____.
3. A pond is a place where fish _____.
